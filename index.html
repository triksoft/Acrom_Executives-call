Who We Are

A student chapter that actually builds things.

ACROM RAS is the ASEB Student Branch Chapter of the IEEE Robotics & Automation Society at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru Campus. We run the workshops, builds, and competitions that turn robotics theory into working hardware.

Every year, a small executive team decides what that looks like — the events we run, the projects we back, the direction we grow in. This year, we're looking for the next one.
